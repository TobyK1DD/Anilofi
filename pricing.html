Yes. £1.99 once, and premium is yours permanently. No subscription, no renewal, no hidden fees. Your account keeps access regardless of how long ago you paid.

What exams does CertReady cover?

CertReady covers both CompTIA A+ exams — Core 1 (220-1101) and Core 2 (220-1102) — with 280 questions per core across all official objective domains.

Do I need to install anything?

No. CertReady runs in your browser and works on any device. You can also add it to your iPhone or Android home screen for an app-like experience — just tap Share → Add to Home Screen in Safari or Chrome.

Can I use it offline?

Once loaded, the question bank and core app work without a connection. Some features like multiplayer and progress syncing require internet access.
