Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026  ·  tobyk1dd.com

1. Who we are

CertReady is operated by TobyK1DD, based in Coventry, UK. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights. Contact us at hello@tobyk1dd.com with any privacy questions.

2. What data we collect

  • Account data — your chosen username, email address, and hashed password when you create an account.
  • Progress data — questions answered, correct/wrong results, bookmarks, and study streaks stored against your account so your progress persists across devices.
  • Payment data — we do not store card details. Payments are handled entirely by Paddle. We receive confirmation of successful payment and your email address to match it to your account.
  • Usage data — basic information about how you use the app (e.g. which exam you practise) stored locally and optionally synced to your account.

3. How we use your data

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To save and sync your study progress across devices.
  • To verify premium access after a successful payment.
  • To improve the question bank and app experience.

We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising.

4. Third-party services

  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider. Your account data is stored securely on Supabase servers. See Supabase's privacy policy.
  • Paddle — our payment processor and Merchant of Record. Paddle processes all transactions and is responsible for payment data. See Paddle's privacy policy.
  • Ably — used for real-time multiplayer sessions. No personal data is sent to Ably beyond a temporary session identifier. See Ably's privacy policy.

5. Data retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you request deletion, we will remove your account and associated data within 30 days. Email hello@tobyk1dd.com to request deletion.

6. Your rights (UK GDPR)

If you are based in the UK or EU you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Object to processing of your data.
  • Data portability — receive your data in a common format.

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@tobyk1dd.com.

7. Cookies

CertReady uses browser localStorage to store your session and preferences locally. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising cookies.

8. Security

Passwords are hashed before storage. All data is transmitted over HTTPS. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, but no system is completely secure.

9. Children

CertReady is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from users under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of CertReady after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
