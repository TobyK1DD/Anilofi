Refund Policy

Last updated: June 2026  ·  tobyk1dd.com

Our policy

CertReady premium is a one-time digital purchase. Because access is granted immediately upon payment, we do not offer refunds once premium features have been unlocked on your account.

Why no refunds?

When you purchase premium access, the full question bank and all features are unlocked instantly and permanently on your account. Unlike a physical product that can be returned, a digital product that has been accessed cannot be un-delivered. This is consistent with standard digital goods policy under UK consumer law (Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, Regulation 37), where the right to cancel does not apply once a digital download or service has begun with the consumer's prior express consent.

Exceptions

We will issue a full refund in the following circumstances:

  • You were charged but premium access was not unlocked on your account due to a technical fault on our side.
  • You were charged more than once for the same purchase.
  • A refund is required by applicable law.

How to request a refund

If you believe you qualify for a refund under the exceptions above, email us at hello@tobyk1dd.com with:

  • The email address used for purchase.
  • Your Paddle order number (found in your payment confirmation email).
  • A brief description of the issue.

We aim to respond within 2 business days. Approved refunds are processed by Paddle and typically appear within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

Technical issues

If you have purchased premium but cannot access premium features, please contact us before assuming you need a refund — this is usually a quick fix. Email hello@tobyk1dd.com and we'll sort it.

Payments processed by Paddle

All payments are processed by Paddle, our Merchant of Record. Paddle's own refund and dispute policies also apply. See Paddle's terms for details.
